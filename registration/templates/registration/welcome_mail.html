In case you're not sure on how the process works, here is a quick recap:

  1. Complete your basic profile information in the portal so that we know more about you.
  2. Check out the currently available audits and apply to those with your preferred dates.
  3. We will contact you and go through your application.
  4. You will be briefed about the details and the audit will be assigned to you if it meets our criteria.
  5. You will perform the audit as discussed and fill out the report on the portal itself.
  6. Submit your report and it will be checked for errors by our QA team.
  7. Once completed, the audit fees will be reimbursed to you within a month.
