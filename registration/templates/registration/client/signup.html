Steps to conduct audit with FloorWalk

1. Sign up with us and setup up your basic details

Create an account with us by entering your company details. Recharge your account and setup process to conduct on ground audits in an automated way

2.Insert questionnaire, store addresses and set up audit dates on the portal

Select from pre-defined templates and customize them according to your need. You can insert store addresses and set timelines for the same

3. Assign audits to the auditors within timelines with specific profiles

Select a relevant auditor profile to conduct audits for your brand. The auditor will conduct audits and FloorWalk will perform Quality checks for the audit

4. View analytics for the audits conducted

Get analytical reports with actionable insights on the portal for the audits conducted on-ground.

FloorWalk will provide real time support in any of the cases that is needed. Real time support on audit conduction will be provided by FloorWalk.
